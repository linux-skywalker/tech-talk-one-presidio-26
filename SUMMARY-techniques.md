# Sharing and scheduling techniques on one RTX 4070: summary

RTX 4070 12 GB, Ubuntu 24.04, k3s v1.36.5, GPU Operator v26.7.2. Numbers are from `logs/techniques/measurements.csv` and the per-technique files. n = runs. Workloads: SmolLM2-135M LoRA training and the Qwen2.5-1.5B vLLM server.

| Technique | Supported on 4070 | Isolation (memory / fault) | Throughput impact (n) | Latency impact (n) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MPS | Yes (device plugin, replicas 2) | Memory: per-pod limit applied, steady pod kept growing while greedy stopped at 5 GiB (n=2). Fault: SIGKILL of one client did not stop the other (n=2); GPU faults not tested | Two jobs 49 s each against 25 s alone, pair no faster than running in sequence (n=2). Long training 117.7 s against 75.9 s alone (n=2) | Server reply 0.847 s next to training against 0.481 s alone (n=2), about 20% lower than time slicing |
| MIG | NO: `nvidia-smi mig -lgip` reports "No MIG-supported devices found", mode `[N/A]` | not applicable | not applicable | not applicable |
| Time slicing | Yes (replicas 2 and 4 gave the same results) | Memory: none; steady pod failed to grow while greedy took the rest (n=2), and a 1.5B fine-tune crashed with out-of-memory next to the server (n=1 plus 4 earlier). Fault: SIGKILL of one client did not stop the other (n=2) | Two jobs 48 to 49 s each against 25 s alone, no gain over sequential (n=2 per setting). Long training 116 s against 75.9 s alone (n=2) | Server reply 1.06 s next to training against 0.475 s alone (n=2 per setting) |
| Kueue | Yes (v0.20.3) | Admission and quota only, no memory or fault isolation | 6 jobs, 3 runs per arm: 6 of 6 finished with Kueue against 3 of 6 (range 2 to 4) without; last job at 498 s against 209 s; 43.4 against 51.5 jobs per hour (the no-queue figure counts only survivors). Earlier single runs: flood 7 of 7 against 3 of 7 | Server answered at 56 s against 208 s (n=1); 6-job runs: server p95 1.85 s with Kueue against 1.40 s without (n=3 each), the server shared the card with training for longer |
| DRA | Yes (driver 0.5.1 accepts the GeForce card; needs device plugin off and driver feature gates for sharing) | Time slicing and MPS claims behave like their device-plugin versions in single runs | No difference from the device plugin (61 s against 62 s for two jobs, n=1 each) | Server p95 1.65 s in the 6-job runs, against 1.85 s with unit-based Kueue (n=3 each); cause not tested |
| DRA + Kueue combined (3 runs each) | Yes, but alpha: Kueue capacity-based quota (gate KueueDRAIntegrationConsumableCapacity) with the driver's consumable memory shares; device plugin off | Admission by GiB of GPU memory; no hardware isolation. Pods asking 3 and 5 GiB shared the card, a 6 GiB pod stayed Pending (n=1) | 6 of 6 jobs, 0 failed pods, last job at 507 s (499 to 519), 42.6 jobs per hour; same as unit-based Kueue (498 s, 43.4), slower than no queue (209 s) | Server p95 1.65 s (1.62 to 1.67) against 1.40 s no queue; jobs waited longer with GPU memory under 9 GB (429 against 78 job-seconds), cause not tested |

Null results worth saying out loud: sharing the card did not make two jobs finish sooner than running them one after the other, replicas 2 versus 4 made no difference, and DRA was not faster than the device plugin, and DRA + Kueue did not finish the 6 jobs faster than Kueue with units.
